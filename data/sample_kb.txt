Enterprise IT Helpdesk Policy Manual

1. Password Reset Policy
Users can reset their domain passwords via the self-service portal at https://password.enterprise.local.
If locked out, contact IT Support at extension 4357 or open a ticket. Passwords must be at least 12 characters
and contain uppercase letters, numbers, and special symbols.

2. VPN Access & Remote Connectivity
Remote access requires Cisco AnyConnect VPN with Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA).
To request VPN access, submit an access request form approved by your department manager.

3. Hardware Upgrades
Laptops and workstations are eligible for hardware refresh every 3 years.
For urgent hardware replacements due to malfunction, contact desktop support.
